# Welcome {.unnumbered}

Thank you for dropping by. This is a work in progress, shared by link, so the text may change as I write.

## Reading it

- **Online:** use the chapter list, or the arrows at the bottom of each page. The search box finds any word in the book, and there's a dark-mode switch at the top.
- **On an e-reader or phone:** use the download button to get the EPUB. It opens in Apple Books, Google Play Books or Kobo, or on a Kindle via *Send to Kindle*.
